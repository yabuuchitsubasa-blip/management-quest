# Product

## Vision
Premium SaaS × AI Agent × RPG × Future Corporate OS.

## Core principle
Management Quest is not an employee surveillance system. It is an AI management agent that protects employees and supports managers' decisions.

## Main product areas
- Team Dashboard
- Member Detail
- AI Analysis / Future HP
- Management Action
- Member / My Status
- Recovery Marketplace

## Core flow
Member → Current HP → Workload → Future HP → AI Analysis → Management Action → Recovery

## Product guardrails
- HP is a workload / condition indicator, not an ability score.
- Recommendations should be explainable in terms of workload signals.
- Manager-facing actions should aim to reduce overload, rebalance work, or improve recovery.
